# Editorial workbench

This folder holds the research and editorial preparation behind 3 AImigo.
The website is English-first with Russian versions; the historical research
archive retains the original Russian wording and research dates.

- [Episode workflow](episode-workflow.md): prepare a discussion, publish a public
  brief, obtain a real speech source, edit the bilingual summary and release.
- [Topic selection for November 2026–January 2027](podcast-prep/2026-11-08-topic-selection/README.md):
  the working package as of 8 November 2026, in Russian, with a per-topic
  analysis, a draft 90-minute plan for each candidate topic, dated search notes,
  the proposed schedule and the questions for the hosts' planning call. Its
  schedule supersedes the one in the dossier package below.
- [13-topic dossier package](research/2026-10-31-3-aimigo-topics/README.md): the
  editorial plan assembled on 31 October–1 November 2026, including evidence,
  counterarguments, host positions and possible guests.
- [Agent autonomy boundaries](research/2026-10-02-agent-autonomy-boundaries.md).
- [Agent payment rights](research/2026-10-09-agent-payment-rights.md) and its
  [historical editorial validation](research/2026-10-09-agent-payment-rights-validation.md).
- [Infrastructure for agent-driven development](research/2026-10-16-agent-development-infrastructure.md).
- [S1E13 announcement](podcast-prep/3-aimigo-s1e13-announcement.md).
- [Existing YouTube chapter copy](youtube-timelines/3-aimigo.md), covering S1E1–2.
- [Migration provenance](migration/README.md) and
  [local workflow decision](adr/0001-local-episode-pipeline.md).

These are public repository documents, not automatically rendered website
pages. The reviewed public subset lives in `content/prep/` and
`content/podcast.json`; active episode packages live in `production/episodes/`.

Research claims remain tied to their stated cutoff dates. Recheck changing
facts and source availability before using them in a new episode. Historical
validation reports record checks performed in polomodov.tech, not checks
performed against this repository. References to original source-tree paths
are resolved in the [historical source index](migration/source-links.md).
